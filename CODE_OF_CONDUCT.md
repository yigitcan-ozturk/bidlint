# Code of conduct

Be professional, specific and respectful.

Engineering and procurement decisions can be safety-, cost- and schedule-sensitive. Disagreement about parsing rules, terminology, standards or decision logic should focus on evidence, reproducible examples and technical trade-offs.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and disclosure of confidential project or vendor information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project constructive and safe.
